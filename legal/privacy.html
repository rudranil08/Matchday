MATCHDAY

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Matchday collects, why, who it’s shared with and the choices you have. It’s run by the Matchday team. Questions or requests: the support link on Matchday’s App Store page. Effective 20 October 2026.

The short version

No ads, no analytics, no tracking and no selling of data. Your leagues, clubs and saved match data stay on your iPhone. Sign-in and the quiz leaderboard use Google Firebase. Your leaderboard name and points are visible to other players.

What we collect

  • Account details. A username and password (accounts kept on this iPhone store only a salted hash of the password, in the iPhone Keychain), or an email address and password (kept by Google Firebase, which stores the password only as a hash).
  • Sign in with Apple or Google. The name and email address Apple or Google share with us. Matchday never sees those passwords.
  • A Firebase user ID. Created when you sign in with email, or when you first open Games with any other account, so your quiz scores can be saved.
  • Quiz results. Your daily points, how many answers you got right, and the name shown on the leaderboard: your username, or your first name and the first letter of your last name.
  • Reports. If you report a leaderboard name, we keep the reported name, its ID and your ID.

What stays on your iPhone

Your leagues and clubs, followed clubs, saved match data, quiz progress, game streaks and settings. Catch me up summaries are written on the device by Apple Intelligence: the match data isn’t sent anywhere for this.

Services we use

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) for email accounts, sign-in and the leaderboard. Data is stored on Google’s servers, which may be in the United States.
  • Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In, if you choose them.
  • Matchday’s own data server, hosted by Cloudflare, provides the tables, results, fixtures, player stats and injuries, which it licenses from Sportmonks. When the app loads them, the server receives your device’s IP address and standard request details, as any website would. It doesn’t receive your account details, and Sportmonks never sees your device.

Why we use it

To run your account, show the leaderboard, keep the service secure and handle reports and abuse. Where EU or UK law applies, we rely on providing the service you asked for (contract) and our legitimate interest in keeping it safe and fair.

Who can see what

Other signed-in players can see your leaderboard name and points. Nobody else sees your email address. We don’t sell or share your personal information for advertising.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Deleting your account in Settings removes your account, your leaderboard name and scores, and the data Matchday saved on your iPhone. Reports are kept as long as needed to deal with them.

Your choices and rights

You can delete your account at any time in Settings › Account › Delete account. You can also ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it, by contacting the support link on Matchday’s App Store page. If you’re in the EU or UK you can also complain to your data protection authority. If you’re in California you have the right to know about and delete your personal information, and we don’t sell or share it.

Children

Accounts, sign-in and the leaderboard are for people aged 13 and over. People under 13 use Matchday as a guest: no account, no email, no Firebase ID and no leaderboard, and everything stays on the iPhone. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact the support link on Matchday’s App Store page and we’ll delete it.

Security

Connections are encrypted. Passwords are never stored in readable form. Sign-in tokens are kept in the iPhone Keychain, on this device only.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
