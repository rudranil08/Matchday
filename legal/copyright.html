Copyright and Content

Players can choose a username, which other players see on the leaderboard. Here’s how to report a name or a copyright problem.

Report a name

In Games, press and hold a name on the leaderboard and choose Report. Reported names are reviewed and removed if they break the Terms of Use. You can also hide a name for yourself straight away.

Copyright notices

If you believe something in Matchday infringes your copyright, send a notice to our designated agent at the support link on Matchday’s App Store page with: your contact details; the work you believe is infringed; what in Matchday you believe infringes it and where it appears; a statement that you believe in good faith the use isn’t authorised; a statement that the notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you’re the owner or authorised to act for them; and your physical or electronic signature.

Counter-notices

If something of yours was removed and you believe that was a mistake, you can send a counter-notice to the same address with your contact details, what was removed, a statement under penalty of perjury that you believe it was removed by mistake, and your consent to the jurisdiction of the courts where you live.

Repeat infringers

Accounts that repeatedly infringe copyright or break the naming rules are banned from posting to the leaderboard.
